One_To_Many ( Unidirectional )
	Question has multiple Answers

it's Unidirectional that means we will come to know for a particular Question , how many and what Answers are there.



One_To_Many-Many_To_One ( Bidirectional )
	Question has multiple Answers and multiple Answers belong to one Question.

it's Bidirectional that means we will not only come to know for a particular Question , how many and what Answers are there but also an Answer/s belong to which Question.
